# Risk Scoring Methodology

1 - Rare:
2 - Unlikely:
3 - Possible:
4 - Likely:
5 - Almost Certain:

## Impact Scale

2 - Minor:
3 - Moderate:

5 - Critical:

## Risk Calculation

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

## Risk Levels

1-4 = Low  
5-9 = Moderate  
10-16 = High  
17-25 = Critical

## Impact Scale

1 - Insignificant: Very little effect on the business and normal operations continue.

2 - Minor: Causes a small disruption, but the business can handle it easily.

3 - Moderate: Causes noticeable damage or disruption, but it is manageable and recoverable.

4 - Major: Causes serious damage, significant disruption, or financial loss and requires prompt action.

5 - Critical: Causes severe damage, major business disruption, or serious legal or financial consequences and requires immediate action.
