# Access Control Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to protect company assets and sensitive information by reducing the risk of unauthorized access to company systems.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, temporary workers, and any other individuals who are granted access to company systems or information.

## Policy Requirements

- All users must use strong passwords that meet company security requirements.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for accounts that access sensitive company systems or information.
- User access privileges must be reviewed regularly to ensure individuals only have access to the systems and information needed for their role.
- Access must be removed promptly for employees, contractors, or other users who are no longer associated with the company.
- 
## Roles and Responsibilities

- Management is responsible for approving and supporting the access control policy.
- The IT/Security Team is responsible for managing user accounts, access permissions, MFA, and regular access reviews.
- Employees, contractors, and other authorized users are responsible for following access control requirements and protecting their login credentials.
